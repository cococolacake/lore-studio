**Lore Studio** is a friendly Windows & Linux app for game masters: keep your world's lore, plan and run sessions, and turn session recordings into transcripts and recaps.

> **Beta.** Everything works and is tested, but expect rough edges. [Report a bug](https://github.com/cococolacake/lore-studio/issues/new/choose) if you hit one.
> New here? On the welcome screen, **Try the demo campaign** builds a small made-up campaign to click around in.

## Start here

- **[Getting started](Getting-started)**: installing, updating and the demo campaign.
- **[Your campaign folder](Your-campaign-folder)**: how a campaign is stored, editing it with other tools, and backups.
- **[Lore and maps](Lore-and-maps)**: pages, links, GM secrets, tags, maps, the connection graph and importing from LegendKeeper.
- **[Sessions](Sessions)**: planning, live mode, recording, transcripts, recaps and posting to Discord.
- **[AI](AI)**: Claude or your own model, recap drafts, loose threads and chat.
- **[World tools](World-tools)**: clocks, handouts, boards, the timeline and the calendar.
- **[Monsters and the party](Monsters-and-the-party)**: the monster maker, the encounter builder and player characters.
- **[Foundry](Foundry)**: the Foundry VTT bridge and scenery.
- **[Player lore book](Player-lore-book)**: a website for your players.
- **[Themes](Themes)**: glass themes, CSS themes and `custom.css`.
- **[Privacy](Privacy)**: what leaves your computer, and when.
- **[Roadmap](Roadmap)**

How Lore Studio was made, and where to ask me questions: see the [AI disclosure](AI-disclosure). Lore Studio is free and open source, forever.

Developing Lore Studio? See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/cococolacake/lore-studio/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
