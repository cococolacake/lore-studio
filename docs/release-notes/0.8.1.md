**Encounter builder** (Monsters page → Encounter)
- Pick monsters, yours or from the free 5e rules, and set how many of each. A meter shows live how hard the fight is for your party (the Dungeon Master's Guide way: monster XP, times a multiplier for how many there are, against your party's thresholds). Change the party levels on the spot to try another group.
- **Copy for a plan**: a summary line with the difficulty, one-click `{actor:…}` Foundry links, and every monster's stat block.
- **Send all to Foundry**, then **Place on scene** puts every monster's tokens, hidden, on the scene open in Foundry.
- Tie an encounter to a session and it shows in Live mode with a **Place** button.
