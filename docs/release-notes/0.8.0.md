**Update the Foundry module too** (Add-on Modules → Update in Foundry, or Settings → Foundry → Install in Lore Studio), then reload Foundry. The new Foundry features need module 0.8.0, and Lore Studio tells you if yours is older.

**Monster maker** (new Monsters page)
- Start from a blank sheet, any of the 334 monsters in the free 5e rules (SRD 5.1, included), a creature in your Foundry world or compendiums, or 5etools (a homebrew file, a link to one, or a creature's JSON).
- A live stat block, and a challenge rating estimate worked out the Dungeon Master's Guide way.
- **Scale** to any CR, or **to your party**: choose how hard the fight should be and how many monsters, and Lore Studio picks the CR from your party's levels. Hit points, AC, attack bonuses, save DCs and damage dice all move, and the monster keeps its character.
- **Send to Foundry** as an NPC with clickable attack and damage rolls (sending again updates it), or **Copy for a plan** to paste it into a session plan as a stat block.

**The party** (new Party page)
- A profile per character: about the player, a backstory synopsis, and your plans for it (GM only: never in recaps, chat or the lore book).
- Link their Foundry character to see class, level, HP, AC and abilities; their levels feed "scale to my party".
- **Fix hit points**: see where a character's max HP comes from (each level-up, Constitution, bonuses, overrides), what's off, and write the fix back to Foundry.
