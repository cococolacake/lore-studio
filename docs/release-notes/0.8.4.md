- **Tags on sessions.** Sessions can have tags now: under the session's title, or in its details (the pencil button). `#tag` searches and the tag list include them.
- **AI tag suggestions for sessions.** The ✨ Suggest button on a session, or the ✨ button on the Sessions list, reads the recap (or the plan, if there's no recap yet) and suggests tags, reusing the ones you already have. They wait under Lore → Tag suggestions, with your page suggestions, until you approve them.
- **Link by tags on the graph.** Each tag that two or more pages or sessions share becomes a small ring, joined to them by dashed lines. Switch it off with **Link by tags** in the side panel. Click a ring to list everything with that tag. Hiding a tag in the legend hides its ring too.
