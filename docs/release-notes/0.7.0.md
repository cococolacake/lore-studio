The first public beta of Lore Studio, a desktop app for game masters. Everything works and is tested, but expect rough edges. Please [report bugs](https://github.com/cococolacake/lore-studio/issues/new/choose).

**Keep your world:** a lore vault of plain Markdown files with tags, links, a graph, header images and AI tag suggestions you approve. Imports from LegendKeeper. Interactive maps with pins (icons, labels, layers, zoom levels).

**Plan and run:** Markdown session plans, live mode, faction clocks, handouts, inspiration boards, a timeline and an in-world calendar (custom months, weeks, eras and as many moons as you like).

**Record to recap:** OBS recording, GPU transcription with speaker labels, then GM and player recaps and loose threads, drafted by Claude or a model on your own computer. Post to Discord. Or switch AI off entirely.

**Foundry VTT:** a bridge module that logs rolls and chat, receives journal pages and handouts, and lets you roll tables, drop monsters, change scenes and play sounds from live mode.

**Share with players:** a player lore book, a static website without the GM-only parts, that you can host anywhere (Netlify Drop works).

New here? Pick **Try the demo campaign** on the welcome screen.
