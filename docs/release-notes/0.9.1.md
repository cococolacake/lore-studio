## 💗 A new theme: Heart Glass
- **Heart Glass** joins the theme list in Settings → Appearance. It's made from the colours of [cococolacake.party](https://cococolacake.party): in dark mode a plum night with pink, magenta, aqua and lilac glow behind the glass, and in light mode a soft blush version of the same.
- Pink buttons use dark text, so labels stay easy to read.

## ⬆️ Update from inside the app
- Lore Studio now tells you when a new version is out: a small **Update** button appears in the sidebar, and **Settings → About** has an Updates card with **Check now**.
- **Update now** brings your copy up to date and restarts Lore Studio with one click. Git folders (GitHub Desktop) are updated with a pull and never overwrite your own changes; downloaded copies get the new release copied over them, keeping your `.venv` and `custom.css`. Your campaigns and settings live elsewhere and are never touched.
- It waits if a transcription or another job is still running.
- You can switch the check off in Settings → About.
