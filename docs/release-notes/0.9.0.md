A fresh new look: Lore Studio now has soft glass themes, a tidier sidebar and a more useful Home page.

## ✨ Glass themes
- **Four themes**, each in light and dark: **Default**, **Cool Glass**, **Warm Glass** and **Lilac**. Light themes are a soft mid-tone instead of bright white, and dark themes are charcoal instead of black.
- **The Geist font** for a cleaner look. You can switch back to your system font in Settings → Appearance → Text.
- **Glass effects**: **Efficient** (the default) shows see-through panels without live blur, so it's very light on your GPU. **Full** adds real blur, and **Off** makes the panels solid.
- Lore pages and the calendar's "today in the world" card get glass headers to match.

## 🧭 Easier to get around
- The sidebar is grouped into **Play**, **World** and **Tools**, with a label under each icon. On smaller windows it scrolls, and the page you're on stays in view.
- **Home** has two tidy columns plus two new cards: **Latest recap** and **Coming up in the world**, which lists the next events and holidays from your in-world calendar.
- Sentence-case titles, and thin scrollbars that only appear when you hover.

## 🪟 Windows
- **Pinning to the taskbar works properly.** A pinned Lore Studio opens Lore Studio itself, with its own icon. (If you pinned it before, unpin it and pin it again once.)
- **The title bar matches your theme** on Windows 11.
- **A new minimal app icon**: the d20 as line art, still clear at taskbar size.

## 🧹 Removed
- The classic pastel themes and the patterned headers. Your setup moves to the Default glass theme automatically and keeps light or dark mode.
