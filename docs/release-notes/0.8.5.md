- **Session date ranges.** In a session's details (the pencil button), **Played on** has an optional second date for a session played over more than one sitting. The in-world date can cover several days too: pick the first day, then **+ Covers several days** to pick the last. It's written as briefly as reads clearly, like "4th – 6th of Deepwinter, 1042".
- A ranged session shows on each of its days in the calendar and stretches across those days on the timeline.
- Dating a session from the calendar page moves it to that day and keeps how many days it covers.
- Without a world calendar, type the in-world date or range however you like, as before.
